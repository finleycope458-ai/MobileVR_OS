What this prototype can and cannot do

It can capture 20 room references, estimate frame-to-frame 2D image movement, read supported orientation sensors, show an AR home launcher, display virtual objects, and detect pinch gestures when hand tracking is enabled.

This is not full 6DoF SLAM. Virtual objects are camera overlays and may drift as the phone moves. Hand tracking needs a visible hand and an internet connection to download its model. Do not walk around while your view is blocked by a headset.
