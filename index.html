What this prototype can and cannot do

It can capture your 20 reference images, save them on this phone, and estimate frame-to-frame 2D image movement. If permitted, it also reads the phone's orientation sensors.

This is not yet full 6DoF SLAM or hand tracking. A single ordinary camera cannot determine reliable real-world distance/scale from these pictures alone. The next stage needs calibrated camera geometry and a real visual-SLAM map; hand tracking is a separate camera-vision module.
